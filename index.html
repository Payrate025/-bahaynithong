Our Story

A Heritage Home
in the Mountains

Bahay ni Thong is more than a place to stay — it is a sanctuary nestled in the cool highlands of Baguio City. Born from a love of Filipino heritage and mountain living, our home blends warm wood interiors, stone accents, and modern comforts into one unforgettable experience.

"We built this space so families and friends could gather, breathe the pine-scented air, and feel truly at home."

Each room is thoughtfully designed to accommodate groups — from intimate family getaways to barkada adventures. With smart locks, full kitchen access, and the gentle hum of mountain breeze, Bahay ni Thong is your home away from home in the City of Pines.
